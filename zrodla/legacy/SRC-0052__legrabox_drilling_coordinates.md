## 8. ASSEMBLY SEQUENCE

### 8.1 Standard Assembly

1. **Mount cabinet profile** to cabinet side panel using fixing positions from Section 1
2. **Attach drawer side** to drawer base and chipboard back
3. **Insert front** in holding position and clip on (tool-free)
4. **Adjust front** using 2D (N) or 3D (M, K, C, F) adjustment in drawer side

### 8.2 Pre-Assembly (Cabinet Profile Before Assembly)

**Important:** If cabinet profile is installed before cabinet assembly:
- Increase fixing position by **+1 mm** from 38 to at least **39 mm** due to tolerances
